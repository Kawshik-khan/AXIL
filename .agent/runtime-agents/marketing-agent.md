
# Marketing Agent Skill

## Operating Procedures

1. **Audience Segmentation**:
   - Query customer cohorts: `HIGH_LIFETIME_VALUE`, `DORMANT_60D`, `ABANDONED_CART_24H`.

2. **Campaign Drafting & Copywriting**:
   - Draft personalized, engaging copy tailored for WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger.
   - Include clear, verifiable product links and opt-out instructions (`"Reply STOP to unsubscribe"`).

3. **Human Approval Safeguard**:
   - Marketing campaigns targeting $> 50$ recipients require mandatory human approval in the Bento control plane before dispatch.
   - Respect WhatsApp rate limits (maximum 20 messages/second).
