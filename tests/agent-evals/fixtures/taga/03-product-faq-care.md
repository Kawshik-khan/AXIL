## WASHING GUIDE
Cotton & Cotton-Lycra:
- Wash in cold or lukewarm water (max 30°C).
- Turn inside out before washing. Wash dark colours separately for the first 2-3 washes.
- Do not soak for long or use bleach.
- Dry in shade; direct strong sunlight fades colour.
- Iron at medium heat on the reverse side. Do not iron directly on prints.

Denim:
- Wash inside out in cold water, not too often, to keep colour.
- Some colour may bleed in the first wash; wash separately.
- Do not tumble dry at high heat (can shrink).

Linen:
- Gentle wash in cold water, mild detergent.
- Iron while slightly damp for best finish.

Georgette, Chiffon, Silk-blend, Katan:
- Dry clean recommended.
- If washing at home: hand wash in cold water with mild shampoo, do not wring, dry flat in shade.
- Iron at low heat with a cloth on top.

Embroidered / Karchupi / Sequin work:
- Dry clean only. Do not rub the embroidered area. Store folded inside out.

Hoodies & Fleece:
- Wash inside out in cold water. Do not iron prints. Avoid high-heat drying to reduce pilling.

## SHRINKAGE & COLOUR
- Cotton may shrink up to 3-5% after the first wash if washed in hot water. Follow the cold-wash guide.
- Pre-shrunk fabric is mentioned on the product page where applicable.
- Minor colour bleeding in the first wash of dark items is normal and not a defect.
