# WARRANTY & GUARANTEE TERMS
Store: TAGA (sample clothing store)
Last updated: 29 October 2026

## QUALITY GUARANTEE
- Every product is checked for stitching, fabric holes, stains, print quality and correct size before packing.
- If something is wrong with the product when it arrives, we replace it or refund it (see Return & Exchange Policy).

## GUARANTEE DURATION
- Clothing (shirts, T-shirts, panjabi, kurti, three-piece, jeans, hoodies): 7-day manufacturing-defect guarantee from the delivery date.
- Zippers, buttons and snaps on jackets/jeans: 15 days from delivery. We repair or replace the faulty part free of charge.
- Accessories (belts, caps, bags, wallets): 30-day guarantee against stitching or hardware failure.
- There is no long-term warranty on clothing.

## WHAT IS COVERED
- Stitching coming open at the seams under normal use.
- Fabric holes or tears present at delivery.
- Faulty zipper, missing or broken button at delivery.
- Print peeling or cracking within the guarantee period after washing as per the care label.
- Wrong size, colour or design compared with the order.

## WHAT IS NOT COVERED
- Damage from washing in hot water, bleach, high-heat ironing or tumble drying against the care label.
- Normal wear and tear, pilling from friction, fading from long sun exposure.
- Alterations or tailoring done after delivery.
- Accidental damage: burns, cuts, pet damage, stains.
- Products bought as "Final Sale" (only a defect at delivery is covered).

## REPLACEMENT CONDITIONS
- Report within the guarantee period with: order number, clear photos of the defect and the care label.
- The product must be returned with the tag (if still attached) and the invoice.
- After inspection (1-2 working days), we send a replacement of the same item. If it is out of stock, you choose another item of equal value, store credit or a refund.
- For a covered defect, we pay the delivery charge both ways.

## HOW TO CLAIM
Step 1: Message on Facebook Page / WhatsApp or call the hotline with your order number.
Step 2: Send photos of the problem.
Step 3: Our team confirms whether it is covered and how to send the item.
Step 4: Replacement or refund after inspection.
