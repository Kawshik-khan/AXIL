# PAYMENT & CASH ON DELIVERY (COD)
Store: TAGA (sample clothing store)
Last updated: 29 October 2026

Note: The numbers and amounts below are samples. Always use the payment number shown in the store's official checkout or confirmation message.

## ACCEPTED PAYMENT METHODS
- Cash on Delivery (COD): all over Bangladesh.
- bKash (Merchant / Payment).
- Nagad (Merchant / Payment).
- Rocket.
- Debit/Credit card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) through the website checkout payment gateway.
- Bank transfer for wholesale/bulk orders.

## bKash PAYMENT GUIDE (Merchant)
Step 1: Open the bKash app, choose "Payment".
Step 2: Enter the merchant number given in your order confirmation message.
Step 3: Enter the exact amount shown in your order.
Step 4: In "Reference", write your order number.
Step 5: Enter your PIN and confirm.
Step 6: Send us the Transaction ID (TrxID) and the last 4 digits of your bKash number.
We never ask for your PIN or OTP. Never share them with anyone.

## NAGAD PAYMENT GUIDE (Merchant)
Step 1: Open the Nagad app, choose "Merchant Pay".
Step 2: Enter the merchant number or scan the QR given in your order confirmation.
Step 3: Enter the amount and your order number as reference.
Step 4: Confirm with your PIN.
Step 5: Send us the Transaction ID.

## ADVANCE DELIVERY FEE RULES
- Inside Dhaka: COD is available with no advance for orders up to 5,000 BDT.
- Outside Dhaka: the delivery charge must be paid in advance via bKash/Nagad before dispatch; the product price is paid in cash on delivery.
- Orders above 5,000 BDT (anywhere): 20% advance payment is required.
- First-time customers with a previous parcel refusal on record may be asked for the full delivery charge in advance.
- The advance amount is adjusted from the total; you pay the rest to the delivery person.

## CASH ON DELIVERY TERMS
- Pay the exact amount to the delivery person; keep change ready if possible.
- You may check that the parcel contains the correct product before paying.
- Trying on clothes in front of the delivery person is not allowed.
- If you refuse a parcel without a valid reason (wrong or damaged product is a valid reason), the delivery charge (both ways) must be paid.
- Repeated refusals may block COD for your number in future orders.
- A COD charge of 1% of the product price may apply for outside-Dhaka orders (sample rule; checkout shows the actual charge).

## PAYMENT CONFIRMATION
- An online payment is confirmed only after our team verifies the transaction ID.
- You will get a confirmation message with your order number and paid amount.
- Wrong amount sent: we will adjust it with your order or refund the difference within 3-5 working days.

## FREQUENTLY ASKED (Bangla)

প্রশ্ন: বিকাশে কীভাবে পেমেন্ট করবো?
