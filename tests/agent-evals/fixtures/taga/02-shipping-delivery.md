# SHIPPING RATES & DELIVERY TIMES
Store: TAGA (sample clothing store)
Last updated: 29 October 2026

Note: The delivery fees below are sample values. The fee charged on an order always comes from the store's delivery settings at checkout.

## DELIVERY CHARGES
- Inside Dhaka city: 70 BDT
- Dhaka sub-areas (Savar, Keraniganj, Gazipur, Narayanganj, Tongi): 100 BDT
- Outside Dhaka (all other districts): 130 BDT
- Parcels above 2 kg: +20 BDT for each extra kg
- Free delivery inside Dhaka on orders of 3,000 BDT or more (sample offer)

## DELIVERY TIME (ETA)
- Inside Dhaka: 1-2 working days
- Dhaka sub-areas: 2-3 working days
- Outside Dhaka (district towns): 2-4 working days
- Remote upazila / hill tracts: 4-6 working days
- Orders confirmed before 5 PM are handed to the courier the same day; later orders go the next working day.
- Delivery can be delayed during Eid, Puja, hartal, floods or heavy rain. We will inform you if that happens.

## COURIER PARTNERS
- Steadfast Courier: main partner for all of Bangladesh.
- Pathao Courier: used for inside-Dhaka express orders.
- RedX: backup partner for some outside-Dhaka areas.
After dispatch you get an SMS with the consignment / tracking ID.

## ORDER CONFIRMATION
- Every order is confirmed by phone call or message before dispatch.
- If we cannot reach you after 2 attempts in 24 hours, the order may be put on hold.
- Please give a correct full address with area, thana and district, and an active phone number.

## CASH ON DELIVERY & PARCEL CHECKING
- You can check that the parcel is the correct product in front of the delivery person before paying.
- Trying on the clothes in front of the delivery person is not allowed; use the exchange policy if the size does not fit.
- If you refuse a parcel without a valid reason, you must pay the delivery charge.

## ORDER TRACKING
- Send your order number to our Facebook Page or WhatsApp to get the latest status.
- You can also track with the consignment ID on the courier's website.
