# RETURN & EXCHANGE POLICY
Store: TAGA (sample clothing store)
Last updated: 29 October 2026

## EXCHANGE TIME WINDOW
- You can request an exchange within 7 days of receiving your parcel.
- The 7 days are counted from the delivery date shown by the courier.
- Requests after 7 days cannot be accepted, except for a manufacturing defect reported with photos (see section 4).

## CONDITIONS FOR EXCHANGE
The product must be:
- Unworn, unwashed and not altered (no hemming, cutting or tailoring).
- With all original tags attached and the original packet/box.
- Free from perfume, makeup, deodorant marks, stains or pet hair.
- Returned with the invoice or order number.

## ITEMS THAT CANNOT BE EXCHANGED OR RETURNED
- Innerwear, undergarments, socks and swimwear (for hygiene reasons).
- Items bought on clearance / "Final Sale".
- Custom-tailored or altered items (e.g. custom-length pants, monogrammed items).
- Free gift items.

## DAMAGED, DEFECTIVE OR WRONG PRODUCT
- Check your parcel in front of the delivery person if possible.
- If you receive a torn, stained, defective, wrong-size or wrong-colour product, inform us within 48 hours of delivery.
- Send: order number, clear photos of the product and the label, and an unboxing video if you have one.
- For our mistake (wrong item / defect), we bear the full delivery charge both ways.

## SIZE EXCHANGE
- One free size exchange per order if the requested size is in stock.
- If you chose the wrong size yourself, you pay the delivery charge for sending the new item.
- If the requested size is out of stock, you can pick another product of equal value or take store credit.

## REFUND OPTIONS
- Store credit (fastest): added to your account within 1 working day after we receive and check the product.
- bKash / Nagad refund: within 3-5 working days after quality check, to the number used for payment or a number you give us.
- Bank transfer: within 5-7 working days.
- Cash on Delivery orders are refunded via bKash/Nagad or store credit.
- Delivery charge is not refundable unless the problem was our mistake.

## HOW TO REQUEST AN EXCHANGE / RETURN
Step 1: Message us on Facebook Page, WhatsApp or call the hotline with your order number.
Step 2: Our team confirms whether the item is eligible.
Step 3: Send the product via courier, or our delivery person collects it while delivering the replacement.
Step 4: After quality check, we send the replacement or process the refund.
